### User Story 1 - Understand Core Concepts (Priority: P1)

As a reader, I want to understand the core concepts of AI-assisted spec-driven book creation so that I can apply them in my own projects.

**Why this priority**: This is the foundational knowledge required to understand the rest of the book.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by reading the chapter and completing the exercises, delivering understanding of AI-assisted SDD principles.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am new to AI-assisted documentation, **When** I read this chapter, **Then** I understand the core principles of spec-driven book creation with AI collaboration
2. **Given** I have read the chapter, **When** I complete the exercises, **Then** I can identify AI collaboration approaches in documentation workflows

### User Story 2 - Identify Target Audience Benefits (Priority: P2)

As a software engineer or documentation engineer, I want to understand how this approach benefits my work so that I can make an informed decision about adopting it.

**Why this priority**: Understanding benefits helps motivate adoption of the approach.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by reading the benefits section and comparing with my current documentation practices.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am evaluating documentation methodologies, **When** I read about AI/Spec-driven benefits, **Then** I can compare them with other approaches
2. **Given** I understand the benefits, **When** I consider my current workflow, **Then** I can identify potential improvements

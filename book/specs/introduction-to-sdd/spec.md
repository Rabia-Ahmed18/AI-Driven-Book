# Chapter Specification: Introduction to Spec-Driven Development

**Chapter**: Introduction to SDD
**Created**: 2025-12-09
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "An introductory chapter that explains the principles and benefits of Spec-Driven Development"

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Understand SDD Principles (Priority: P1)

As a reader, I want to understand the core principles of Spec-Driven Development so that I can apply them in my own projects.

**Why this priority**: This is the foundational knowledge required to understand the rest of the book.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by reading the chapter and completing the exercises, delivering understanding of SDD principles.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am new to SDD, **When** I read this chapter, **Then** I understand the core principles of spec-driven development
2. **Given** I have read the chapter, **When** I complete the exercises, **Then** I can identify spec-driven approaches in code examples

---

### User Story 2 - Learn SDD Benefits (Priority: P2)

As a developer, I want to understand the benefits of SDD so that I can make an informed decision about adopting it.

**Why this priority**: Understanding benefits helps motivate adoption of the approach.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by reading the benefits section and comparing with my current development practices.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am evaluating development methodologies, **When** I read about SDD benefits, **Then** I can compare them with other approaches
2. **Given** I understand the benefits, **When** I consider my current workflow, **Then** I can identify potential improvements

---

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Chapter MUST explain the core principles of Spec-Driven Development
- **FR-002**: Chapter MUST provide concrete examples of spec-first workflows
- **FR-003**: Chapter MUST include exercises for readers to practice SDD concepts
- **FR-004**: Chapter MUST reference the book's constitution and governance principles
- **FR-005**: Chapter MUST provide clear definitions of SDD terminology

### Key Entities

- **Specification**: A formal document that describes requirements before implementation
- **Implementation**: The actual code or system built according to the specification
- **Validation**: The process of ensuring implementation matches the specification

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Reader can explain 3 core principles of SDD after reading the chapter
- **SC-002**: Reader can identify spec-first vs implementation-first approaches in code examples
- **SC-003**: Reader completes at least 2 exercises from the chapter
